# Study design

The historical inventory contains 471 national country–crop systems for maize, rice, soybean and wheat, with 25 physical ENSO events during 1984–2022. Each comparison retains its own timing, observation and event-support criteria; a system inventory does not imply universal eligibility. Chinese rice and wheat, Indian wheat and Argentine soybean are withheld where resolved calendars are needed.

The study first compares El Niño and La Niña with majority-neutral years, then examines contrary events and severe low yields. Independent agricultural conditions organize environmental comparisons. Five weather factors describe fitted yield variation; period changes in their shares and strong-versus-ordinary event yield contrasts remain separate comparisons. Matched harvest years connect yield and harvested area to reported production. Finally, the dated outlook supports conditional 2027 risk estimates for 200 eligible systems.

The country-level scale cannot resolve all within-country agricultural differences. The many crop observations share only 25 physical climate events. These features constrain interpretation and do not support treating records as independent ENSO realizations.
