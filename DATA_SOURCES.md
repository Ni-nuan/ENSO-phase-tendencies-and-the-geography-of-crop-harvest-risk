# Data sources

Only selected national/system-level processed results and a public-domain basemap are redistributed. Obtain raw products from their providers; do not substitute newer releases or current outlooks when reconstructing the historical study.

| Product | Version/time used and scientific role | Access and redistribution |
|---|---|---|
| FAOSTAT Crops and livestock products | Historical outcomes 1984–2022; boundary observations 1982–2024 for centred references; information available through 2024 for the 2027 reference | [FAOSTAT](https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#data/QCL). Derived yield, harvested-area and reported-production results are included; raw global bulk download is excluded. [FAO statistical terms](https://www.fao.org/contact-us/terms/db-terms-of-use/) generally apply CC BY 4.0 with attribution and third-party exceptions. |
| NOAA RONI | Event registry fixed 18 September 2026; 25 physical events, seasonal agricultural assignment | [NOAA RONI](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso/roni/). Processed event/support tables are included; no replacement with later classifications. |
| MIRCA2000 | Crop calendars and irrigation background; fixed seasonal geometry, not annual observed phenology | [Portmann et al. 2010](https://doi.org/10.1029/2008GB003435). Raw grids/calendar archive excluded; obtain from the provider under its terms. |
| SPAM 2020 V2r2 | Crop-area spatial allocation for crop-season aggregation | [Provider](https://mapspam.info/) and [dataset DOI](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/SWPENT). Raw grids excluded; provider terms govern them. |
| GPCC monthly precipitation | Version 2025, crop-season precipitation and sensitivities | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5676/DWD_GPCC/MONTHLY_V2025_050). Processed country/crop summaries only; no raw gridded product redistributed. |
| CRU TS temperature | TS4.09, historical seasonal temperature comparison | [CRU](https://crudata.uea.ac.uk/cru/data/hrg/) and [product description](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-020-0453-3). Raw grids excluded. |
| ERA5-Land | Temperature, dewpoint-derived VPD, upper-metre soil moisture and shortwave radiation, historical five-factor model | [Product description](https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-13-4349-2021), [CDS](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/). Only aggregated/model-result tables included; raw reanalysis excluded. |
| CHIRPS | v3 monthly precipitation, alternative weather-source comparisons | [CHC](https://data.chc.ucsb.edu/products/CHIRPS/v3.0/). Raw gridded files excluded. |
| IRI ENSO outlook | Issued 19 October 2026; information cutoff 8 November 2026. Printed phase probabilities and unallocated neutral/La Niña mass define the dated 2027 scenario | [IRI](https://iri.columbia.edu/our-expertise/climate/forecasts/enso/). This URL changes over time; per-system dated probabilities are preserved in [Table 12](results/supplementary_tables/Table_12/). No current outlook is retrieved by the supplied risk functions. |
| NOAA CPC and ERSSTv5 | Observed/outlook context available by 8 November 2026 and traditional Niño3.4 phase reference | [CPC](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso_advisory/ensodisc.shtml), [ERSSTv5](https://doi.org/10.1175/JCLI-D-16-0836.1). Current pages are not the historical snapshot; large raw files excluded. |
| Natural Earth | 1:50m country boundaries for display | [Terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/): public domain. The basemap archive is included for figure reconstruction, with credit to Natural Earth contributors. |

The processing and model outputs are research transformations, not official provider products or endorsements. Project licensing remains separate from upstream rights. Gridded products with unverified redistribution conditions are not bundled.
